03

How I work with AI

I use AI tools in every project, mostly to research and build faster. Below: my steps, the tools, and one case where you can check the split yourself.

  1. 01

    Start on site

    At the client's job site, or as a guest in the restaurant.

    Me problem definition

  2. 02

    Research

    Perplexity for sources, Gemini for comparisons, ChatGPT for structuring strategy, Manifold for marketing and social data.

    AI gathering Me selection

  3. 03

    Flow and states

    I list the steps and the states before the build. Example: my brief for this portfolio.

    Me the list

  4. 04

    Build

    Claude Code turns the list into a working page or prototype. Animations in GSAP and HyperFrames, rendered to MP4 with Playwright. 3D in three.js.

    AI the code

  5. 05

    Visuals

    Higgsfield and Leonardo AI for images and video, Canva for posts and materials.

    AI variants Me selection

  6. 06

    Review

    Before anything goes to the client, I check it against the brief and the brand.

    Me sign-off

AI Where AI speeds me up

  • Code for pages, prototypes and animations.
  • Market and competitor data with sources.
  • Drafts of long documents and their structure.
  • Image and video variants to compare.
  • Dictation instead of typing.

Me What I decide

  • What the problem is and who the user is.
  • Which states and edge cases the product must handle.
  • Whether a visual fits the brand. Basko's realistic 3D ad did not, so I dropped it.
  • What a client's material may show: no client logos without consent, no personal data.
  • Whether the result is ready for the client.

Where I said no to the AI

  1. Basko Clean ad AI A video model produced a realistic 3D version of the ad. Me I rejected it and made flat 2D motion graphics with kinetic type and the logo's cleaner figure. Why: the realistic look did not fit the brand.
  2. Next example Content pending: a second case where I rejected or changed an AI output

Tools I use and what I made with them

  • Client websites for Basko Clean and Livingroom, the interactive 3D model for KSK Pretor, brand animations in code and this portfolio. I also use it for strategy and research.

  • Copy, research and the strategy work behind the KSK Pretor plan for the German market.

  • Research and side-by-side comparisons for client projects.

  • Research with sources and market analysis for client strategy.

  • Video and images for ads, generated with models such as Seedance, and video edits with person replacement.

  • Generated graphics for client marketing.

  • Marketing and social media research for client profiles.

  • AI agent (chatbot) prototypes for the KSK Pretor shooting range.

  • Graphics, social media posts and marketing materials for clients.

  • Dictation and voice work: I write by speaking.

  • Brand animations for KSK Pretor in code: the logo intro and outro in Polish and German, rendered frame by frame to MP4.

  • The interactive 3D model for KSK Pretor, built in code from simple shapes.

Case 06

This portfolio

I planned and designed this site and built it in Claude Code. I tested every version for accessibility, layout and links, and refined it over several review rounds. The test scripts and the QA log are in the repository (opens in a new tab).

What the checks and reviews caught QA log (opens in a new tab)

  • ScrollSection links landed about 160 px too low: the smooth-scroll library counted the CSS offset twice.
  • PhoneLong headings made the page wider than a 360 px screen.
  • FactsThe fact check flagged sentences that claimed more than my sources. They were rewritten or marked “Content pending”.

What I'm exploring now
